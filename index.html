01 MISSION

Building toward a sustainable future.

Energy is fundamental for developing economies to industrialize. Climate change is the opposing force. I want to leverage engineering, economics, and data analytics to design energy systems that resolve that tension, allowing us to grow while mitigating the environmental cost.

That means working at the intersection: materials and carbon technologies that perform, and the economic case that gets them deployed at scale.

02 RESEARCH

My research interests.

R.01 Material Science Engineering Computational modeling of processes and designing of materials to produce sustainable energy, fuels, and chemicals with improved efficiency and durability that drives economic growth while minimizing environmental impacts. R.02 Carbon management & energy transition Advancing predictive models of multiphase flow in porous media to support CO2 capture, transport, and geological storage initiatives to develop scalable, economically viable solutions for a low-carbon future.  R.03 Techno-economic analysis Applying techno-economic modeling to evaluate whether emerging energy and carbon technologies can scale from lab-stage innovation to commercially viable real-world deployment.
