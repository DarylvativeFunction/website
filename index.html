Building toward a sustainable future.

Energy is fundamental for developing economies to industrialize. Climate change is the opposing force. I want to leverage engineering, economics, and data analytics to design energy systems that resolve that tension, allowing us to grow while mitigating the environmental cost.

That means working at the intersection: materials and carbon technologies that perform, and the economic case that gets them deployed at scale.

Technical innovation and economic thinking, working together, can solve large-scale energy challenges.

My research interests.
